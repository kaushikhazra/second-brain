# 6 — the brain wanders when its owner is away, if the owner switched it on

## Goal

> Every acceptance criterion in GitHub issue #6 holds against this repo's skills: curiosity
> is asked about once, on a fresh brain's first session and on the first session after
> `VERSION` changes, and a decline gives one hint and silence until the next version; it
> is switched with `/curiosity on|off|status` and the record survives a restart and is
> git-ignored; it fires only from the heartbeat's quiet-cycle observation while active and
> never while the owner is in conversation; one pass starts from memory, reads one outside
> source and cites it, stores through `/create-memory`, lays provisional edges at negative
> strength, writes a dated record, and stops after one root and one wander; and a check
> exists for each criterion that fails when the behaviour is removed.
