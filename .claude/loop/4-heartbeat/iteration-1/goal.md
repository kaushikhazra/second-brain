## Goal

> heartbeat is three files, `SKILL.md`, `observe.md` and `goal.md`, paired by id; each

> admitted only with a date, still open, and a durable-enough type; the self map is never
> written by a beat; and a check exists for each criterion that fails when the behaviour
> is removed.
